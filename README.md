Ten years keeping production systems up, the last four of them on blockchain infrastructure: Ethereum
and Solana nodes, RPC and WebSocket gateways, and the multi-cloud plumbing underneath them.

Most of what I do is the unglamorous half of reliability. Error budgets that actually constrain what
ships. Alerts that fire for something a human can act on at 3am. Post-mortems that end in a merged
change rather than a document. Infrastructure you can rebuild from a repository instead of from memory.
